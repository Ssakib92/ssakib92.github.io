## Neuromorphic Computing
<div class="research-card">
<p class="research-focus"><strong>Focus:</strong> Brain-inspired computation and efficient learning</p>

I study spiking neural networks and biologically inspired learning mechanisms, 
with emphasis on STDP-based learning, energy efficiency, and hardware-aware modeling.
</div>

## Spintronic and Emerging Memory Devices
<div class="research-card">
<p class="research-focus"><strong>Focus:</strong> MTJ modeling and stochastic device behavior</p>

My work includes stochastic LLG-based modeling of magnetic tunnel junctions, 
with applications in true random number generators and probabilistic hardware.
</div>

## Device-Aware Optimization and Inverse Design
<div class="research-card">
<p class="research-focus"><strong>Focus:</strong> Optimization under physical constraints</p>

I apply evolutionary algorithms and inverse design techniques to optimize 
device parameters and system-level performance under realistic constraints.
</div>

## Applied Machine Learning for Physical Systems
<div class="research-card">
<p class="research-focus"><strong>Focus:</strong> ML guided by physics and data</p>

I develop machine learning models for physical systems, including time-series 
forecasting, simulation-driven learning, and hybrid data–physics approaches.
</div>
